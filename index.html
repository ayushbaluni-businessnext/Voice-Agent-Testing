Seven things it actually does

Each of these is a testable behaviour, not a slogan. The interesting part is what happens when a caller interrupts mid-sentence.

🎙

Streaming speech recognition

Transcribes as you speak, with partial results — so it can react before your sentence ends.

✋

Barge-in and recovery

You talk over it, it stops, listens, and picks the thread back up instead of restarting.

🧠

Intent, not keywords

Separates what you want from how you said it — "cancel that" and "actually, leave it" land differently.

🔧

Tool calling mid-turn

Looks up an order, books a slot, files a ticket — inside the conversation, then reports back aloud.

🌐

Code-switching

Hindi, English and the mix of both in one sentence, without asking you to pick a language.

⏱

Sub-second turn-taking

Target under 500 ms from end of your speech to its first syllable. Latency is the product.

🤝

Clean handoff

When confidence drops, it briefs a human with the transcript and the reason — no repeating yourself.

🔒

Grounded answers

Retrieval over your own policy and product data, with the passage cited — not improvised.

📈

Every call measured

Deflection, containment, escalation reason and time-to-resolution, per agent version.
